# Shipping FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Orders inside the EU arrive in 2-4 business days. Espresso machines ship from Lisbon; grinders
and accessories ship from Rotterdam, so a mixed order may arrive in two boxes.

## Which carrier do you use?

Parcels under 5 kg travel with a national postal carrier; heavier parcels, including every
espresso machine, travel with a courier that requires a signature on delivery.

## My parcel is late

Tracking can lag a day behind the parcel. If tracking has not moved for 5 business days,
contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.
