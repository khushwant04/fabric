# Refund policy

## Damaged or defective items

Items that arrive damaged in delivery qualify for a full refund, including shipping, when the
damage is reported within 14 days of delivery. Attach a photo of the damage and of the box.
We do not ask customers to pay for return shipping on damaged items; a prepaid label is sent
with the refund ticket.

## Change of mind

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days. Return shipping is
paid by the customer and a 10% restocking fee applies to opened espresso machines.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the returned item
reaching our warehouse. Store credit is issued immediately when the customer prefers it.
